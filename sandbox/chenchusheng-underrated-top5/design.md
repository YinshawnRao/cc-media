# 陈楚生最被低估的5首歌 — Design

## Format

- Vertical short-form video, `1080x1920`, 30fps.
- Narrated countdown reveal order: 5 -> 1, following the user's ranking in reverse.
- Female narration (`zf_xiaoyi`) for intro, every song transition, project outro, and fixed CTA.
- Each song section: narration over ducked music bed -> short breathing beat -> continuous song showcase.
- The final spoken sentence is the repository fixed CTA.

## Visual Identity

- Mood: midnight livehouse, understated singer-songwriter, synth haze, lonely city light, mature and restrained.
- Background: real MV/Live footage where available; default letterbox with blurred fill. Do not crop aggressively unless a probe proves the singer remains intact.
- Palette:
  - base: `#07090c`
  - panel/scrim: `rgba(7,9,12,.72)`
  - text: `#f2eee6`
  - muted text: `#c7bdad`
  - accent: `#6fc3c0` (cold teal, synth/livehouse feel)
  - rank-1 accent: `#d6b36a` (aged gold for first-place reveal)
  - warm accent: `#b96f62` (rust, used sparingly for memory/age motifs)
- Type: serif display for song names and poetic statements; readable CJK sans for notes and labels. Heavy enough for mobile video.
- Motion: slow light sweeps, quiet page-slide entrances, blur crossfades, small breathing zoom. Avoid variety-show stickers, neon overload, and meta production copy.

## Composition Rules

- Cover frame is complete at t=0 and usable as a thumbnail.
- Song cards stay in the lower third; mini labels stay top-left during showcase.
- Full-music windows must be continuous singing sections, not a montage of random fragments.
- No watermark / URL / platform ID / prompt / local path on screen after crop and overlay.
- Screen text may show ranking and song information, but not production mechanics.
- Project outro summarizes Chen Chusheng's underrated side without asking for votes; the fixed CTA handles interaction.
