# 世界杯最佳主题曲 TOP5 Design

## Direction

Sports-broadcast energy with archival music-video texture. The frame should feel like a premium football countdown: stadium floodlights, pitch green, trophy gold, broadcast red, and clean editorial typography.

## Palette

- Background: `#07100b`
- Deep panel: `#0d1a13`
- Foreground: `#f5f0df`
- Muted text: `#b7b6a5`
- Trophy gold: `#d8aa45`
- Broadcast red: `#e33d2f`
- Pitch green: `#1d8f5a`
- Cool line: `#d8e6e0`

## Typography

- Display: `Noto Serif SC`, 700/900
- UI and captions: `Noto Sans SC`, 500/700/900
- Rank numerals and metadata: `JetBrains Mono`, 700/900

## Motion

- Main transition: fast broadcast block wipe in gold/red/green.
- Song cards enter with a firm slide and slight scale settle.
- Full-song showcase keeps labels small and stable; the footage should breathe, not be covered by text.

## Constraints

- Do not reveal the full song list in the opening.
- Voiceover should identify rank and World Cup year, while song/artist details are visual.
- Keep full-volume music sections free of narration.
- No platform watermarks, URLs, prompt text, paths, or internal project labels on screen.
