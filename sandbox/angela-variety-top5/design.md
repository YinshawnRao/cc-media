# 张韶涵的综艺歌曲TOP5 - Design

## Format

- 1080x1920 vertical short video.
- Ranking is shown in the user's order: 1 to 5, not countdown.
- Footage carries the emotion; graphics should frame the stage, not bury it.

## Visual Direction

- Mood: polished variety-show recap, dark stage base, sharp rose-gold highlights.
- Palette:
  - Background black: `#08070b`
  - Panel ink: `#121019`
  - Primary rose: `#ff3b87`
  - Warm gold: `#f5c56b`
  - Soft white: `#f8f4ef`
  - Muted text: `#b8aeb7`
- Typography:
  - Chinese sans: `Noto Sans SC`, fallback `PingFang SC`.
  - Ranking numerals: heavy weight, rose/gold gradient.
- Motion:
  - Opening: quick title hit, then settle.
  - Song cards: lower-third slides in during narration, shrinks during full-song showcase.
  - Transitions: 0.5-0.7s dark wipe with rank number and show/date metadata.

## Audio Rhythm

- Female narration `zf_xiaoyi`.
- During narration: source music is a low bed.
- After each narration: 0.9-1.2s digestion/swell, then a short full-volume stage highlight.
- Final video must use muxed `master.wav`, not HyperFrames-rendered audio.
