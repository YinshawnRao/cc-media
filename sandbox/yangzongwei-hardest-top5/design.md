# Design Direction

- Mood: restrained ballad documentary, dim stage light, old MV texture, emotional but not melodramatic.
- Palette: near-black `#07080b`, warm ivory `#f6efe3`, amber `#c99b55`, muted blue gray `#7f9aa5`, deep red accent `#b83f46`.
- Typography: Chinese serif for song titles and emotional statements; sturdy sans for labels and notes; numeric rank in a compact mono style.
- Motion: slow push, soft focus, small amber light sweeps; transitions should feel like turning from one memory to the next.
- Avoid: neon variety-show colors, full ranking reveal in the opening, meta text such as "倒数开始", visible URLs or platform watermarks.
