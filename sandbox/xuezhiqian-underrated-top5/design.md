# Design — 薛之谦最被低估的5首歌

## Mood

冷感、克制、带一点舞台雾气和深夜自省。不要综艺花字，不要热闹彩色榜单；这期的关键词是“遗珠”“回望”“少年感”和“藏起来的痛”。

## Palette

- Background: `#070A0E`
- Panel: `#111720`
- Foreground: `#F3EFE6`
- Muted text: `#B9B4AA`
- Accent: `#8FB5C8`
- Warm accent: `#D4A15F`
- Rank one gold: `#F0C77B`
- Danger red: `#A8463E`

## Typography

- Display / song title: `Noto Serif SC`, 800-900
- UI / narration labels: `Noto Sans SC`, 500-900
- Numbers: `JetBrains Mono`, 700-800

## Motion

- Reveal cards should feel like old film title cards emerging from haze.
- Showcase footage should stay continuous and restrained, with a short swell into full volume.
- Use blur crossfades and opacity/scale reveals, not loud glitch transitions.

## Avoid

- No neon cyber palette.
- No generic centered PPT title pages.
- No dense explanatory text on screen.
- No visible source URLs, paths, prompts, or platform watermarks.
