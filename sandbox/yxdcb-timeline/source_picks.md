## yt-dlp 命令模板
```bash

yt-dlp --cookies /Users/yinshawnrao/explorer/cc-media/sandbox/www.youtube.com_cookies.txt \
  --download-sections "*HH:MM:SS-HH:MM:SS" --no-playlist \
```
