# 童安格最被低估的5首歌 — Design Direction

## Mood

文雅、克制、旧时代情歌、夜色里的遗憾感。画面像旧唱片内页和电影片尾字幕，不做综艺花字，不做高饱和霓虹。

## Palette

- Background: `#071014` 深蓝黑
- Panel: `#10191d` 暗墨绿
- Foreground: `#f4ead8` 旧纸暖白
- Muted: `#aeb8b0` 灰绿
- Accent: `#d9a85f` 灯光琥珀
- Secondary accent: `#7da6a1` 水色灰青
- Shadow: `#030506`

## Typography

- Song titles: `"Noto Serif SC", "Songti SC", serif`
- Labels and narration captions: `"Noto Sans SC", "PingFang SC", system-ui, sans-serif`
- Ranking numbers and metadata: `"JetBrains Mono", "SFMono-Regular", monospace`

## Motion

- Primary motion: slow focus pull, soft blur crossfade, film-grain drift.
- Ranking reveal: measured slide + scale, not punchy slam.
- Decorative motif: one lamp glow, water ripple rings, faint vertical film scratches.

## Avoid

- Do not use purple/blue neon gradients.
- Do not make the video look like a karaoke lyric screen.
- Do not show URLs, platform watermarks, file paths, prompts, or source IDs in the final frame.
- Do not over-crop old 4:3 footage; preserve the source frame unless a watermark or lyric band must be removed.
