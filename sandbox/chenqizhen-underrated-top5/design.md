# 陈绮贞最被低估的5首歌 — Design

## Format

- Vertical short-form video, `1080x1920`, 30fps.
- Narrated countdown reveal order: 5 -> 1. Female voice (`zf_xiaoyi`).
- Opening shows the topic and emotional premise only, not the full ranking.
- Each song section: female transition narration -> breathing beat -> continuous music showcase.
- Ending reveals the full 5 -> 1 list, then the fixed CTA as the final spoken line.

## Visual Identity

- Mood: literary indie, diary pages at night, quiet city lights, cassette-era warmth, a little sharp and lonely.
- Background: official MV footage, default letterbox with blurred fill. Do not crop aggressively unless a probe proves the singer remains intact.
- Palette:
  - base: `#090b0b`
  - panel/scrim: `rgba(8,10,10,.72)`
  - text: `#f4f0e8`
  - muted text: `#c8c1b5`
  - accent: `#9fc5aa` (dusty sage, intimate and literary)
  - rank-1 accent: `#d9b56f` (aged gold for the first-place reveal)
  - danger accent: `#b76e5f` (rust, used sparingly for decay/time motifs)
- Type: serif display for titles/song names, readable CJK sans for notes. Heavy enough for mobile video.
- Motion: quiet page-slide entrances, soft blur crossfades, mild breathing zoom on cover, no loud neon or variety-show stickers.

## Composition Rules

- Cover frame is complete at t=0 and usable as a thumbnail.
- Song cards must not cover the singer's face; keep labels in the lower third and small tags during full-music windows.
- Music showcase window should stay continuous and long enough to feel like a real listening moment.
- No watermark / URL / platform ID / prompt / local path on screen.
- Screen text must not mention production mechanics beyond ranking itself.
- Final summary lists 5 -> 1: 5 距离, 4 太聪明, 3 躺在你的衣柜, 2 80%完美的日子, 1 腐朽.
- Project outro must only summarize Chen Chi-chen's artistic character. It must not ask for votes; fixed CTA handles interaction.
