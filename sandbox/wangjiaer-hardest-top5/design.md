# 王嘉尔最难的5首歌 — Design

## Format

- Vertical short-form video: 1080x1920, 30fps.
- Countdown order follows the brief: reveal from No. 5 to No. 1.
- Opening must not expose the full ranking. Use cover-style title plus work description only.
- Female narration throughout intro, each transition/song setup, and outro.
- Each song gets a continuous representative showcase segment, not fast-cut fragments.
- Final audio is pre-mixed as `master.wav` and muxed after HyperFrames render.

## Visual Identity

- Mood: dark, cinematic, smoky stage/MV texture.
- Base colors: near-black `#07070a`, charcoal `#121217`, warm off-white `#f4f0eb`.
- Accent colors: crimson `#ff2f4f`, amber `#f0b35b`, pale steel `#9aa3b2`.
- Type: local CJK system sans fallback, high weight for rank/song cards.
- Motion: controlled pushes, fades, slow scale. No playful stickers, no ranking list in the opening.

## Structure

1. Cover: title + short premise, no full ranking list.
2. No. 5 `Come Alive`: jazz/rock/R&B mixed groove.
3. No. 4 `LMLY`: retro pop lightness and control.
4. No. 3 `Cruel`: dark tension and grunge control.
5. No. 2 `Blow`: rock-smoke vocal pressure.
6. No. 1 `Made Me a Man`: broken texture, head voice, emotional release.
7. Outro: summarize the ranking list and the core reason these songs are hard.
