# 那英最被低估的5首歌 — Design

## Format

- Vertical short-form video, `1080x1920`, 30fps.
- Narrated countdown reveal order: 5 -> 1. Female voice (`zf_xiaoyi`).
- Opening shows the topic and emotional premise only, not the full ranking.
- Each song section: female transition narration -> breathing beat -> continuous music showcase.
- Ending reveals the full 5 -> 1 list, then the fixed CTA as the final spoken line.

## Visual Identity

- Mood: late-90s/early-00s Chinese pop, classic diva, dim MV light, cassette-era melancholy, restrained but emotionally sharp.
- Background: official MV footage when available. Default letterbox with blurred fill. Do not crop aggressively unless probes prove the singer remains intact.
- Palette:
  - base: `#08070a`
  - panel/scrim: `rgba(5,5,8,.72)`
  - text: `#f8f3ec`
  - muted text: `#bdb3a6`
  - accent: `#d6b16a`
  - rank-1 accent: `#c7333f`
  - secondary accent: `#7fb7b2`
- Type: serif display for titles/song names, readable CJK sans for notes. Heavy enough for mobile video.
- Motion: slow film-title entrances, blur crossfades, soft pressure-line reveals, mild breathing zoom on cover, no loud variety-show stickers.

## Composition Rules

- Cover frame is complete at t=0 and usable as a thumbnail.
- No full song list/ranking in the opening.
- Song cards must not cover the singer's face; keep labels in the lower third and small tags during full-music windows.
- Music showcase window should stay continuous and long enough to feel like a real listening moment.
- KTV lyric/title sources may use full-width crop and bottom masks; masks must sit below labels.
- No watermark / URL / platform ID / prompt / local path on screen.
- Screen text must not mention production mechanics beyond ranking itself.
- Final summary lists 5 -> 1: 5 船, 4 你是我的人, 3 一万一千公里, 2 白丝线, 1 愿赌服输.
- Project outro must only summarize Na Ying's vocal character. It must not ask for votes; fixed CTA handles interaction.
