# Design Notes

- Format: 1080x1920 vertical countdown.
- Tone: bright, sweet, nostalgic, but not a single-pink palette.
- Palette: plum base, candy pink accent, honey yellow for highlights, mint for contrast, cream text.
- Visual layer: official MV footage full-screen with dark scrim, subtle grain, rank cards, mini labels during showcase, and a final ranked list.
- Audio rule: render visuals with HyperFrames, then mux the pre-mixed `master.wav` afterward.
