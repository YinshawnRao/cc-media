# Design — 飞儿乐队最被低估的5首歌

## Mood

Dark cinematic music countdown. Early-2000s fantasy rock, desert ruins, live-stage amber light, belief and distance. The piece should feel like uncovering hidden tracks from an old but still glowing music universe.

## Palette

- Background: `#070912`
- Foreground: `#F4EDDF`
- Muted text: `#C7BEAD`
- Accent amber: `#D6A95F`
- Weathered teal: `#72A7A5`
- Finale gold: `#F2CD73`

## Typography

- Display Chinese: `Noto Serif SC`, weights 800-900.
- UI/body Chinese: `Noto Sans SC`, weights 500-800.
- Use large serif song titles and oversized ghost ranking numbers.

## Motion

- Reveal cards should feel like old gates opening: slow ghost rank breath, title rising, then footage pushes through.
- Showcase clips should not be over-edited. One continuous song window per entry.
- Transitions: soft black/blur dissolves with amber emphasis; #1 can feel brighter and more resolved.

## Avoid

- No platform names, URLs, file paths, prompt/meta text, or source labels in-frame.
- No one-note purple-blue neon palette.
- No aggressive vertical crop unless the subject is stable and single-person.
- No fast montage that cuts the music short.
