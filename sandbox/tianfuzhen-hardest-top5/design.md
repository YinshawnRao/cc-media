# 田馥甄最难的5首歌 — Design

## Format

- Vertical short-form video, `1080x1920`, 30fps.
- Countdown order: 5 -> 1.
- Opening must not reveal the five-song ranking.
- Each song section: female narration -> breathing beat -> continuous chorus/showcase.
- Ending reveals the final ranking summary.

## Visual Identity

- Mood: restrained, literate, cool, high-pressure vocal analysis.
- Background: real MV/live footage, letterboxed unless crop is proven safe.
- Palette:
  - base: `#08080b`
  - panel/scrim: `rgba(7,7,11,.70)`
  - text: `#f6f1ec`
  - muted text: `#b9b1aa`
  - accent: `#d8b06a`
  - rank 1 accent: `#e45d73`
  - cool accent: `#8eb8c8`
- Type: system CJK stack, heavy display weights, letter spacing 0.
- Motion: clean slides, subtle marker-line reveals, no blobs/orbs.

## Composition Rules

- Cover: title and theme only; do not list all songs at the start.
- Song cards use the countdown number and one compact difficulty statement.
- Showcase sections keep only a small top-left rank tag.
- Captions and overlays must not include prompts, paths, source URLs, or internal notes.
- Final summary lists: 1 讽刺的情书, 2 你就不要想起我, 3 魔鬼中的天使, 4 悬日, 5 矛盾.
