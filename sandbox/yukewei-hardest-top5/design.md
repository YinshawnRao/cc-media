# 郁可唯最难的5首歌 — visual direction

## Mood

影视 OST 的夜色感。克制、温柔、疲惫，但排名推进要有清晰的倒数节奏。

## Palette

- Background: `#07080b`
- Ink: `#f4efe8`
- Muted text: `#d7cec1`
- Gold accent: `#d8b06a`
- Rose accent: `#b84a62`
- Deep panel: `#0f1117`

## Typography

- Display: `Songti SC`, `STSong`, serif
- UI/body: `PingFang SC`, `Hiragino Sans GB`, system-ui, sans-serif
- Numerals: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace

## Motion

Slow editorial reveal, soft focus, no comic impact. Rank one may use rose accent; all other ranks use gold.

## Avoid

- No platform names, URLs, file paths, prompts, or production notes on screen.
- No aggressive vertical crop. Keep the source footage complete unless removing burned-in text.
- No bright variety-show sticker style.
