# Typography

Choose fonts for the subject, language coverage, readability and available local font files. No banned-font list, forced serif/sans pairing or extreme weight ratio. One family or several can work when hierarchy is clear.

Use offline project WOFF2 with relative @font-face. Do not assume the compiler will fetch a font just because it is named. Historical local() projects retain their documented machine/OS boundary.

Check actual Chinese and Latin glyphs, line breaks, clipping and target-size reading. Semantic manual breaks are allowed; avoid isolated characters or broken names. Establish identity prominence through composition, size, weight or contrast rather than a strict universal largest-font condition.
