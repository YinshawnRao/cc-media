# Frame composition

Use the topic, footage and viewer's reading conditions to choose density and hierarchy. design.md may specify layout as well as brand; respect the user's choices. Keep important text readable, avoid obscuring subjects, and verify actual crops and keyframes.

A scene may be a clean full-frame MV, a sparse title, or a dense explanatory graphic. Decoration and movement should support the content; no mandatory element count, metadata accents, texture, color count or continuous movement. Static backgrounds and deliberate holds are valid.

Use adequate text size and contrast at the target output resolution. Ratios and pixel values are starting points; judge the rendered result. A single clear focal point is often sufficient. Flex, grid and absolute positioning are all available. Deliberate line breaks are useful for Chinese semantic phrases.

First frame is immediately usable as a cover. For later scenes choose cuts, fades or other transitions based on continuity and pace, with seek-safe timing and no unintended blank frames.
